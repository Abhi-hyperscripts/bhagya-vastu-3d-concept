Ancient Wisdom Explained

What Each Science Does for You

Understand the science, purpose, and real-world benefits of each Vedic discipline — and how they work together.

Vastu Shastra

Vastu maps the five elements — Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space — to specific directions in your home. When these elements are in their correct zones, they create a natural current of positive energy supporting every area of life.

  • North zone governs wealth, career opportunities, and financial flow
  • South-West controls stability, relationships, and long-term health
  • East zone influences social connections, health, and new beginnings
  • North-East is the divine zone — controls spiritual growth and clarity
  • Kitchen in wrong direction causes health issues and relationship conflicts

Vedic Astrology

Jyotish — "Science of Light" — is the oldest predictive science on earth. Your birth chart (Kundli) maps the sky at the exact moment you were born, revealing personality, karma, strengths, and the timing of major life events.

  • Lagna (Ascendant) defines your core personality and life purpose
  • Dasha periods determine when specific planets activate in your life
  • Raj Yoga combinations indicate wealth, authority, and success potential
  • Manglik Dosha affects marriage — needs proper matching and remedies
  • Kaal Sarp Yoga creates delays — specific mantras and rituals remove it

Numerology

Numerology recognises that the universe is fundamentally mathematical. Every name, date, and number carries a specific frequency. When your name's vibration aligns with your birth number, you experience a natural flow of opportunity and abundance.

  • Life Path Number (birth date total) — your soul's core mission this lifetime
  • Expression Number (full name) — your natural talents and abilities
  • Destiny Number — the opportunities and challenges written into your path
  • Name correction changes the energetic frequency others feel from you
  • Business name aligned to owner's number generates natural attraction

Frequently Asked

Common Questions Answered

Everything you want to know before booking your consultation with Bhagya Vastu.

Can Vastu be corrected without demolition or major structural changes?

Yes — absolutely. The vast majority of Vastu corrections can be achieved without any structural breaking. Kkomal Vasisht specialises in non-demolition Vastu remedies, using strategic placement of furniture, mirrors, crystals, colours, and sacred yantras to correct directional imbalances and restore positive energy flow throughout your space.

How accurate is Vedic Astrology and what can a Kundli analysis reveal?

Vedic astrology is a mathematically precise system mapping nine planetary positions at your moment of birth. A thorough Kundli analysis can reveal your core personality traits, karmic patterns, natural strengths, timing of career peaks, periods of financial growth, relationship compatibility, health tendencies, and the specific windows when major life events are most likely to occur.

Can online Vastu or astrology consultations be as effective as in-person visits?

Yes. For Vastu, a detailed floor plan with accurate measurements and compass directions provides everything needed for a thorough analysis. For astrology and numerology, your birth details are sufficient. Bhagya Vastu's online clients across India and abroad have achieved the same quality of results as NCR in-person clients.

What is the difference between Vastu Shastra and Feng Shui?

Both are ancient spatial sciences, but from different civilizations and using different frameworks. Vastu Shastra is the original Vedic science from India — 5,000+ years old — grounded in the Panchabhuta (five elements) and cosmic directions defined in Sanskrit texts. Vastu predates Feng Shui and uses a more detailed directional system with 16 zones versus 8 in Feng Shui.

How quickly can I expect results after implementing the remedies?

Results vary based on severity of the imbalance and how many remedies are implemented. Many clients report a noticeable shift in energy and mental clarity within 2–4 weeks. Significant life changes typically become apparent within 1–6 months of properly implementing the recommended remedies.

Is numerology connected to astrology in the Vedic tradition?

Yes — deeply. In Vedic numerology (Ankjyotish), each number 1–9 is ruled by a specific planet from the Navagraha: 1 is Sun, 2 is Moon, 3 is Jupiter, 4 is Rahu, 5 is Mercury, 6 is Venus, 7 is Ketu, 8 is Saturn, and 9 is Mars. This creates a direct bridge between your numerological profile and your astrological chart — making combined readings uniquely powerful.
